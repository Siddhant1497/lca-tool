Questions we get asked

Before you get in touch.

Which rating system should we choose?

It depends on who you need to convince. Multinational tenants and global investors usually ask for LEED. IGBC is widely recognised in the Indian market, GRIHA is often specified for public buildings, and EDGE is the quickest and cheapest route and links well with green finance. The free review gives you a recommendation for your project.

Who will actually work on our project?

The founder leads every project personally. Senior specialists in modelling, carbon, MEP or reporting join when the project needs them. You won't be handed to a junior team.

Do you only do certification?

No. Architects and MEP teams hire us for energy, daylight and microclimate studies alone, and companies hire us for GHG inventories, LCA and ESG reporting with no building certification involved.

Do you work outside India?

Yes. The founder has worked on projects in Malaysia, Vietnam, Cambodia, Thailand and France, and much of our carbon and reporting work is done remotely.

What do we need to send for the free review?

Whatever you have: drawings, an area statement, a brief or a short description. If you're at concept stage, a site plan and the building type are enough to start.

How are your fees set?

After the review you get a written proposal with a fixed scope. We run lean, without a large office or idle staff, so our fees pay for the work rather than overheads.
