About

A new firm with seven years of practice behind it.

India has always known how to build for heat, from the jaalis of the Hawa Mahal to Kahn's shaded brick at IIM Ahmedabad. VERDEAXIS exists to bring that thinking back, and to prove it with numbers.

Founder

Siddhant Jain

Founder and Principal Consultant · IGBC AP · EDGE Expert & Auditor

Siddhant has hands-on experience in more than 200 certified green building projects across India and Malaysia, covering retail, offices, stores and logistics parks.

Alongside certification, he advises multinational organisations on GHG reporting and builds decarbonisation roadmaps to meet climate targets. He works with GRESB, the GRI Standards and the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and runs the energy, daylight and LCA models himself. He has worked on projects in India, Malaysia, France, Vietnam, Cambodia and Thailand.

How we're built

A network of specialists, not a pyramid of staff.

The founder leads every project. Senior specialists join as each project needs them: energy and daylight modellers, LCA and carbon analysts, MEP and commissioning engineers, ESG reporting specialists, façade consultants and local partners abroad. No idle bench, no big office, no junior hand-offs. That's how we keep fees lower.
