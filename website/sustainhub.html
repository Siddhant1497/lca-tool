SustainHub · built by VERDEAXIS

One workspace for a client's carbon, certification and LCA.

SustainHub brings the GHG inventory, the decarbonisation plan, the certification portfolio, the disclosures and the building LCA into one place. Our clients get live numbers and a shared dashboard instead of a PDF that's out of date the week it's sent.

What's inside

Six modules, switched on as the client needs them.

Carbon calculator

Scope 1, 2 and 3 activity data by site, with data-quality tags. Emission factors come from bundled official packs: the CEA CO₂ Baseline Database for India's grid, UK DESNZ 2025 and 2026, and GHG Protocol GWPs. Every factor keeps its source, edition and row.

Decarbonisation strategy

SBTi-style near-term and net-zero targets, a trajectory to net zero, the gap by scope, a library of 27 measures linked to LEED credits, and a Now, Next, Later roadmap.

Command Center

KPIs, the Scope 1 and 2 pathway against a 1.5 °C line, Scope 3 hotspots, a marginal abatement cost curve, intensity trends and the certification pipeline.

Disclosures

Report-ready tables for the GHG Protocol, GRI 305, BRSR Principle 6 and IFRS S2, with prior-year comparisons and a status on every line.

Certification portfolio

LEED, IGBC, GRIHA, EDGE, WELL and Fitwel projects with category scorecards and a "needs attention" list.

Building LCA

Material inventory against EPD data, EN 15978 stages A to D, LEED and BREEAM checks. The A1 to A3 result goes into Scope 3 in one click.

For clients

Share a dashboard, not your spreadsheet.

Each client gets a separate workspace. When it's time to share, we build a read-only dashboard with only the sections, frameworks and projects you choose. Activity data and factor libraries never leave the workspace.

  • Runs in the browser, with no backend: your data stays on your machine
  • Formatted Excel workbook export, CSVs and full backups
  • Disclosures map to each framework's references; it isn't an assurance review
