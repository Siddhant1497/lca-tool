01 · Energy modelling

Know the energy bill before the building exists.

We build a whole-building energy model of your design and a code baseline beside it, then test the changes that move the result: glazing, shading, insulation, HVAC system, controls and on-site renewables. You see the saving and the payback of each one before you commit.

What we model

  • Baseline and proposed models under ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G (Performance Rating Method) and the ECBC/ECSBC whole-building method
  • HVAC systems: chilled water, VRF, DOAS, district cooling, with sizing, part-load curves and unmet-hour checks
  • Energy conservation measures one by one, with energy cost savings and simple payback
  • Renewable energy contribution and net-zero energy feasibility
  • BEE star rating and EPI benchmarking for existing buildings

02 · Daylight and glare

Bright rooms, without glare or extra heat.

In India, more glass usually means more cooling. We find the window size, glass and shading that bring daylight deep into the floor plate without overheating it or blinding the people sitting by the window.

What we model

  • Climate-based daylight: spatial daylight autonomy (sDA) and annual sunlight exposure (ASE) on an hourly weather file
  • Daylight factor and illuminance studies for IGBC and GRIHA
  • Glare probability (DGP) at real seating positions
  • Façade options: louvres, fins, jaalis, light shelves and glass specification, tested side by side
  • Views and visual comfort for WELL and LEED

Used for

LEED EQ DaylightIGBC daylightingGRIHA visual comfortWELL Light

03 · Microclimate and CFD

Outdoor spaces people can actually use in May.

For campuses, townships and masterplans, the space between buildings matters as much as the buildings. We model sun, wind and heat at street level so landscape and massing decisions are made on evidence.

What we model

  • Outdoor thermal comfort using UTCI and PET, hour by hour through the hot season
  • Pedestrian wind comfort and safety around towers and podiums
  • Natural ventilation potential and airflow through courtyards and atria (CFD)
  • Urban heat island: shading, surface materials and planting compared
  • Solar access and overshadowing for site planning

Used for

MasterplansIGBC and GRIHA site creditsLEED heat islandDesign reviews

04 · Life cycle assessment

Know the carbon in your materials.

Concrete and steel can account for most of a new building's lifetime carbon before anyone switches on a light. We measure it, find the biggest hotspots, and test lower-carbon alternatives against a reference design.

What we deliver

  • Whole-building LCA across life-cycle stages A1 to D, to EN 15978 and ISO 14040/14044
  • Baseline against proposed comparison across six impact categories
  • Embodied carbon hotspots and material substitutions, such as blended cement and recycled steel
  • Product LCA and EPD support for manufacturers (EN 15804, ISO 14025)
  • A1 to A3 results fed straight into Scope 3, category 2

Used for

05 · Carbon and decarbonisation

A carbon number you can defend, and a plan to bring it down.

We build your Scope 1, 2 and 3 inventory with traceable emission factors, set targets that follow the science, and rank the measures by what they cost per tonne saved.

What we deliver

  • GHG inventories to the GHG Protocol Corporate and Scope 3 standards and ISO 14064-1
  • Location- and market-based Scope 2, with India grid factors from the CEA CO₂ Baseline Database
  • SBTi-style near-term and net-zero targets, with a year-by-year trajectory
  • Marginal abatement cost curves and a phased roadmap of measures
  • Product carbon data for EU CBAM declarations and monitoring support under India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme

  • CDP climate questionnaire responses

07 · Green building certification

The right rating, achieved without surprises.

We pick the system that fits your market, budget and timeline, set a target you can actually reach, and run the whole process: registration, credit strategy, documentation, design reviews, submission and reviewer responses.

Systems we deliver

  • LEED (USGBC / GBCI): BD+C, Core and Shell, ID+C, O+M
  • EDGE (IFC / GBCI): design and final certification, plus EDGE audits
  • IGBC (CII): new buildings, green factories, homes and interiors
  • GRIHA: GRIHA V-2019 and Large Developments
  • WELL and Fitwel: health and wellbeing strategy and documentation

A plaque earns nothing by itself. We help you use it.

Most consultants stop at the award. We help owners turn the rating into rent, sales and cheaper finance, and make sure the building performs the way the model said it would.
