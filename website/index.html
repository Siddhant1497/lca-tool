What we do

We close the gap between what the rules ask for and what the building actually does.

Most projects treat sustainability as paperwork at the end. By then the façade, the HVAC system and the structure are fixed, and the only thing left to optimise is the documentation.

We work the other way round. We model the building early, test the options with real numbers, and then take it through certification. For companies, we do the same with carbon: measure it properly, set a credible plan, and report it in the format your regulator or investor expects.

Why now

India once built for heat by instinct. Now the rules demand proof.

A carbon market for heavy industry, assured ESG data for listed companies, tougher building codes and an EU border tax. Each one lands on someone's desk as a deadline.

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur, 1799 · Photo: Adityaoberai, CC BY 4.0

2025

Carbon Credit Trading Scheme

Emission-intensity targets for 490 plants in eight sectors, including cement, aluminium, refining and textiles. Shortfalls are covered by buying carbon credits.

In force
2026

EU CBAM

Exporters of steel, aluminium and cement must back their products with verified emissions data, or be charged on default values that usually cost more.

Definitive phase
2026

BEE star rating for large buildings

Proposed: new buildings of 20,000 m² or more need at least a 3-star rating or an approved equivalent.

Draft, July 2026
2027

BRSR Core

India's top 1,000 listed companies report assured ESG data, with value-chain disclosures phasing in for their suppliers.

Phasing in
2035

How a project runs

First call to certificate, in five steps.

  1. Scope

    Brief, goals, target rating and the right system. Free.

  2. Analyse

    Site visit, data audit, energy and daylight models, carbon baseline.

  3. Document

    Credit documents, compliance drawings and model files.

  4. Submit

    Upload, reviewer queries and appeals, handled by us.

  5. Certify and use it

    Award, handover, then marketing, leasing and finance support.
