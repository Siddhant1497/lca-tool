03 · What we do

Four things,
done properly.

01

Measure

Energy, daylight and microclimate models, LCA and EPDs, GHG inventories for Scope 1, 2 and 3, and CBAM and CCTS data.

LEED, EDGE, IGBC, GRIHA, WELL and Fitwel, from feasibility to award. ECBC, ECSBC and BEE star ratings. EDGE audits.
