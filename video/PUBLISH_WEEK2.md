## Mon: Is your AC set too cold?

**YouTube title:** Why You Should Set Your AC to 24°C (BEE Rule Explained)

**Description:**
Since January 2020, star-labelled room ACs sold in India must ship with a default setting of 24°C (Bureau of Energy Efficiency). BEE estimated that moving from 20°C to 24°C can cut an AC's electricity use by about 24%. Setting it colder doesn't cool the room faster; the AC just runs longer, chasing a colder target. Start at 24, use the ceiling fan, close curtains on the sunny side, clean filters every few weeks in summer, and service the unit before the heat arrives.

Sources: Ministry of Power / BEE (PIB, 2018 advisory; 2020 default-setting requirement for star-labelled room ACs).

## Wed: IGBC AP or LEED AP?

**YouTube title:** IGBC AP vs LEED AP: Which Green Building Credential for a Career in India?

**Description:**
IGBC AP comes from the Indian Green Building Council (part of CII). It's built around Indian practice, and the exam isn't tied to a single rating system; students and freshers can start with IGBC AP Associate. LEED AP comes from GBCI, the body that certifies LEED: a specialty credential (such as BD+C or O+M) that builds on the LEED Green Associate. For mostly Indian projects and clients, IGBC AP is a practical start; for international firms or multinational clients, LEED carries more weight. Many consultants hold both. Check current exam formats and fees on igbc.in and usgbc.org before booking.

## Fri: How to read an EDGE certificate

**YouTube title:** How to Read an EDGE Green Building Certificate (Certified, Advanced, Zero Carbon)

**Description:**
An EDGE certificate shows three savings compared with a typical local building: energy, water and embodied energy in materials. EDGE Certified needs at least 20% on all three; EDGE Advanced needs at least 40% energy savings; EDGE Zero Carbon adds renewable energy to cover the rest (check the current EDGE protocol for exact rules). Read the three numbers, not just the badge. And note the stage: a design-stage certificate is a prediction; the final certificate means an auditor checked the site.

Sources: EDGE (IFC) certification levels and protocol. EDGE is a trademark of IFC; this video is not affiliated with or endorsed by IFC or any EDGE certification provider.
