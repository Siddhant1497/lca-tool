**Title:** Every Building Has a Carbon Story. Most Claims Skip 3 Chapters 📖 #shorts

**Description:**
A building's carbon isn't just the energy it uses. It's written into four chapters: materials, construction, life in use, and the end. Many "green" claims count only one of them. A life cycle assessment (LCA) reads the whole book.

Chapters, as defined in EN 15978:
• A1–A3 Materials · A4–A5 Construction · B1–B7 Use · C1–C4 End of life · D Reuse and recycling

Sources:
• IEA (report for the 2022 G7 presidency): steel ≈ 8%, cement ≈ 7% of global CO₂
• WorldGBC, Bringing Embodied Carbon Upfront (2019): upfront carbon is expected to be about half of new construction's carbon footprint between now and 2050
• EN 15978: life-cycle stages for buildings
