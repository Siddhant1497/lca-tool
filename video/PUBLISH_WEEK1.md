## Fri: What is an EPD?

**Upload to:** LinkedIn first (native, Friday morning when procurement and design teams are online), then YouTube Shorts.

**YouTube title:** What Is an EPD? Environmental Product Declarations Explained in 60 Seconds

**YouTube description:**
An EPD (Environmental Product Declaration) is like a nutrition label for a building product. It reports the product's impacts, such as its carbon footprint, measured with a life cycle assessment. For construction products it follows EN 15804 (a Type III declaration under ISO 14025) and is checked by an independent verifier. Most EPDs are valid for five years: check the date and the declared unit (per kg, m² or m³). LEED rewards products with EPDs, and whole-building LCA tools use them as data. When a supplier says "low carbon", ask to see the EPD.

Episode 3 of Green Building in 60 Seconds.
References: ISO 14025; EN 15804. Validity is set by each EPD programme; check the document.
