> versus: OPERATIONAL | EMBODIED
Net zero what? Operational, or embodied?

# scene photos=solar_roof grade=day
> label: Operational = energy used every day @ every day
A building can be net zero on the energy it uses every day, and still carry a large carbon debt in its concrete, steel and glass from the day it was built.

# scene photos=car_road grade=day
Most "net zero" claims only count the first one. That's not exactly dishonest. It's incomplete. Like a car ad bragging about mileage that says nothing about how the car was made.

# scene photos=nutrition_label grade=day
> item: Operational carbon @ operational
> item: Embodied (upfront) carbon @ embodied
> item: Ideally: whole-life carbon @ whole-life
The most credible projects report both numbers, ideally as whole-life carbon. If a project only shows you one, ask about the other.

# scene photos=new_green_building grade=day zoom=out

Not saying don't trust green buildings. Ask which carbon they're counting, like reading the ingredients, not just the front of the box.
