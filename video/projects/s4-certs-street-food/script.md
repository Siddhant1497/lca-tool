Four green building certifications in under a minute: who made them, who they're for, and how to pick one.

Facts:

- EDGE: created by IFC (World Bank Group); EDGE Certified needs ≥20% savings in energy, water and embodied energy in materials vs a local baseline; third-party site audit before final certification.
- IGBC: Indian Green Building Council, part of the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII).
- GRIHA: developed by TERI and adopted by MNRE as India's national rating system; mandated for many central government / PSU buildings.
