# 4-week content calendar and niche promotion plan

## The problem, and the way round it

Green-building content has a small search audience. So don't measure it like an entertainment channel, and don't wait to be found. Use three levers:

1. **Bridge topics.** Start from something everyone feels (a hot flat, the electricity bill, a heatwave, buying a home) and land on your expertise. These reach people who would never search "embodied carbon".
2. **Searched-intent topics.** A few narrow searches have steady, motivated traffic: exam prep (LEED Green Associate, IGBC AP), careers ("how to become a sustainability consultant"), and compliance ("what is BRSR", "what is an EPD"). Few creators answer them well.
3. **Distribution, not discovery.** Put every video in front of an audience that already exists: LinkedIn, industry WhatsApp groups, IGBC/ASHRAE/student chapters, architecture colleges, client teams.

**What counts as success:** enquiries, connection requests from developers and manufacturers, invitations to speak, and exam students who follow you into their careers. One developer enquiry is worth more than 100,000 views on a spam channel.

## The 12 videos

Format: the paper style (`/paper-video`), 45–70 s, plain English, hook in the first second, the fix at the end. **Verify** = check the fact against its source before scripting.

|---|---|---|---|

| 1 | **LEED Green Associate: 5 things to know before the exam** | Searched (exam) | Current exam format and fees. Verify on usgbc.org |
| 1 | **What is an EPD? In 60 seconds** | Searched (compliance) | EN 15804 / ISO 14025. Verify standard references |
| 2 | **Is your AC set too cold? The 24 °C rule** | Bridge (bills) | BEE guidance on the 24 °C default AC setting. Verify the current BEE notification |

| 3 | **Why white roofs cool homes** | Bridge (heatwave) | Indian cool-roof programmes, e.g. Telangana's cool roof policy. Verify the current policy |
| 3 | **More glass ≠ more daylight** | Expertise (simulation) | Glare and heat gain trade-offs. Your DesignBuilder experience; use a generic example |
| 3 | **BRSR in 60 seconds: what listed companies must report** | Searched (compliance) | SEBI BRSR scope and the BRSR Core assurance timeline. Verify on sebi.gov.in |
| 4 | **WELL vs Fitwel: what makes a building healthy** | Searched + expertise | Who runs each (IWBI; Fitwel / CAFH). Verify current ownership |

**Timing hooks to reuse each year:** the summer heatwave (April–June: heat, AC and cool-roof topics), World Environment Day (5 June), World Green Building Week (September), COP (November), and the dates of new BEE, SEBI or ECBC rules (post within days of the news).

## Promotion routine (about 30 minutes per video)

**Before posting**
- Pick the one community that most needs this video. Exam videos go to student groups and IGBC/USGBC chapters. Compliance videos go to ESG and CFO circles on LinkedIn. Bridge videos go to general audiences: Reels, WhatsApp, residents' groups.

**On posting day**
1. **LinkedIn:** upload the MP4 natively, with a 3-line post: the hook, one takeaway, then a question. Tag only people or organisations the video genuinely concerns, and only where it's appropriate.
2. **YouTube Short + Instagram Reel** with the same captions.
3. **Send it personally to 5 people** who'd find it useful (a client, a site engineer, a student, a colleague) with one line on why. Direct shares beat hashtags.
4. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Turn the best question into next week's video.

**Every week**
- **Answer 2 real questions** on Quora, Reddit or LinkedIn with a full text answer. Link the video only as "I made a 60-second explainer".
- **One LinkedIn carousel** from the best-performing video.
- **Check Qwoted** or journalist requests for green-building or ESG topics.

**Every month**
- **Collaboration:** a joint video or guest slot with an architecture or construction creator, a college department or a manufacturer. Explain their topic with your expertise.
- **Hindi or Hinglish version** of the top video. It reaches a much larger Indian audience.
- **Review:** which videos brought enquiries or followers, not just views. Make more of those.

**Always on**
- Playlist link in your email signature, LinkedIn Featured section and proposals.
- The LCA tool link in every description (once you've made its page public).
- The same series name and look on every video, so people recognise it at a glance.

## Realistic expectations

- **Months 1–3:** a few hundred to a few thousand views per Short is normal for this niche. The useful signal is who watches: developers, students and peers.
- **Months 3–6:** the searched-intent videos (exams, BRSR, EPDs) keep collecting views steadily. One or two bridge videos may break out, because heat and bills reach everyone.
- **Ongoing:** consistency beats volume. Two good videos a week, every week, beats 21 a week for a month.
