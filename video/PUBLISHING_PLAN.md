### Ep 1: The concrete under your feet
**Title:** What Is Embodied Carbon? The CO₂ Hidden in Concrete and Steel
**Description:**
Embodied carbon is the CO₂ released making, transporting and building with materials like cement and steel, before anyone moves in. Cement and steel alone produce around 15% of global CO₂, roughly seven times all of aviation.

The fix exists: blended cements (fly ash, slag), mass timber where it fits, and recycled steel.

### Ep 2: Why "net zero" can mislead you
**Title:** Is a "Net Zero" Building Really Net Zero? Operational vs Embodied Carbon
**Description:**
Most "net zero" building claims cover operational carbon, the energy used every day. Many leave out embodied carbon, which is already emitted by the time the building opens. Ask which one a claim covers. The most credible projects report both, ideally as whole-life carbon (EN 15978).

### Ep 4: Your AC is a climate supervillain
**Title:** Why Your AC's Refrigerant Can Be Worse Than Its Electricity (R-410A GWP)
**Description:**
R-410A, a common AC refrigerant, traps about 2,088 times more heat than CO₂ (IPCC AR4, 100-year GWP). If a typical home system's full charge escapes, that's roughly a year of driving. A heat pump gives 3 to 4 units of heat per unit of electricity; whether it pays back depends on your climate and energy prices.

Episode 4 of The Carbon You Can't See. ◀ Previous: Renovate or rebuild? · ▶ Next: LEED vs EDGE vs IGBC vs GRIHA

### Ep 5: LEED vs EDGE vs IGBC vs GRIHA
**Title:** LEED vs EDGE vs IGBC vs GRIHA: Which Green Building Certification to Choose?
**Description:**
LEED (USGBC) carries the most international recognition. EDGE (IFC, World Bank Group) requires at least 20% savings in energy, water and embodied energy in materials. IGBC (part of CII) is built around Indian codes and climate. GRIHA (TERI) is India's national rating system. None is universally better: pick the one that fits the project. The food comparisons are opinion.

### Ep 6: What a green building audit finds
**Title:** What Happens in a Green Building Audit? Site Verification Explained
**Description:**
A green building site audit checks that what was built matches what was certified on paper. Most of the job is finding the gap between the drawings and what was installed, like a sensor on the plan but not on the ceiling, or a fixture swapped late to save cost. Certification isn't a trophy. It's proof that something that could easily have been cut, wasn't.
